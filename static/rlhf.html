TEACH THE ROUTER

A tiny demonstration of reinforcement learning from human feedback.

Route A Route B Router's current best (toggle) Persona's true best (after reveal) Main road Park River Road bridge (counts as main road) Footbridge
  1. Press Deal a persona. Read it with your group, then hide it. Don't tell other groups.
  2. Each round the router proposes two cycling routes from ● to ■. Pick the one your persona would prefer.
  3. The router never sees your persona, only your clicks. It fits a reward model to them, then plans with that reward.
  4. After about 15 rounds, press Show router's best route, swap screens with another group, and try to guess their persona from the route and the bars alone.
  5. Then Reveal persona to compare what the router learned against what the persona actually wanted.
