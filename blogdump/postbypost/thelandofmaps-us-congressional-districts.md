<p>“”“Eliminate Gerrymandering”““<br/></p><p>These kinds of maps are interesting visualizations, but I guarantee you that these maps would exhibit such <i>strong </i>partisan &amp; racial bias. Thus, it burns me up to read that people think this is what Larry Lessig (or Barack Obama in the SOTU) mean when they talk about reducing/eliminating gerrymandering.<br/></p><p>It’s strange to me when people forget that gerrymandering is boundary manipulation <i>for the benefit of some group! </i>That’s the key; both that a district distorts the underlying human geography <i>and </i>it does so in a way that has political consequences. It’s not <i>just</i> boundary manipulation. <br/></p><p>Even if it were only about boundary manipulation, squiggly/elongated districts <i>are not necessarily manipulated! </i>Human habitation, land use, and the physical geography of places don’t always fit well inside of a voronoi tiling&hellip;</p>
