<p>I’ve got your back, though. The newest release, <code>cenpy 0.7</code>, now supports <code>n</code>-column queries by chunking through 50-coulumns at a time. Be careful, though, as I&rsquo;m sure if you pull down the entire Census 2010 short form in one go, you will probably get rate limited.</p>
