This is a map of the Polsby-Popper metric for US Congressional districts. In essence, people use it to identify how &ldquo;normal&rdquo; a political district&rsquo;s shape is, with larger values being more &ldquo;regular.&rdquo; It&rsquo;s the same as used in a washington post piece. My methodology differs a bit from their published map, and I&rsquo;d love to figure out how to get the neat little hover info on the WaPo map.
<br/>
But, with this down, I don&rsquo;t know why I would use this over folium, since I know how to make the maps myself&hellip;</p>
