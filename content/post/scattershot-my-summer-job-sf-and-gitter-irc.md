Title: Scattershot: My (summer) Job, SF, and Gitter + IRC
---<p>Finally landed on my feet in the upstairs of a cozy duplex in Bernal Heights, waiting for my &ldquo;onboarding&rdquo; for <a href="https://nextdoor.com" target="_blank">Nextdoor</a> after Memorial Day. So far, everything&rsquo;s been nice. I was worried I&rsquo;d be living in a very yuppie neighborhood, but, at first brush, Bernal Heights seems to have held onto a sense of localness.</p>

<p>Which brings me to the question of how to answer &ldquo;Are you local?&rdquo; I heard this asked to a few people today, eavesdropping across bars and coffeeshops during my first day of urban orienteering. Obviously, I&rsquo;m not a San Franciscan. But, at the same time, I&rsquo;m not really a tourist, either. Stating that you&rsquo;re there for the summer usually leads to the typically American &ldquo;What do you do?&rdquo;, which is usually a discussion I tend to avoid. It&rsquo;s interesting that a city with significant conflicts over <a href="http://www.nytimes.com/2015/05/23/us/high-rents-elbow-latinos-from-san-franciscos-mission-district.html?_r=0" target="_blank">sustainable housing development, aging housing stock, and gentrification</a> has so far fought harder to determine the locality of the people I&rsquo;ve been eavesdropping on than any other I&rsquo;ve been in.</p>

<p>Fortunatley, Bernal Heights, <a href="https://www.google.com/maps/@37.7419017,-122.4160503,15z" target="_blank">sandwiched between</a> the &ldquo;Real&rdquo; Mission and the &ldquo;Outer&rdquo; Mission, appears to be a little less embattled. I&rsquo;m very glad this is the case. There are probably a ton of human &amp;amp; economic geography theses just on space-time analysis of things like Zillow prices, hedonic models on cafes and bus stops, and analyses of the social text. It&rsquo;d be super interesting to see some of this work.</p>

<p>And, as a total aside, I just learned about <a href="https://gitter.im" target="_blank">gitter</a>, a chat service centered around <a href="https://github.com" target="_blank">github</a> repositories. I&rsquo;ve hated pretty much every webchat application except for <a href="https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/hangouts/nckgahadagoaajjgafhacjanaoiihapd?hl=en" target="_blank">Hangouts</a>, so I was very excited to learn that gitter has an <a href="https://irc.gitter.im" target="_blank">Internet Relay Chat API</a>. Being a member of a few IRC communities, this is very cool; every github repo gets a free irc channel, with webchat integration like found on <a href="https://www.mibbit.com/" target="_blank">mibbit</a>. So, if anyone&rsquo;s interested, I&rsquo;ll be adding <a href="https://gitter.im/pysal/pysal" target="_blank">pysal/pysal</a> on gitter to the list of channels I hang in.  </p>
